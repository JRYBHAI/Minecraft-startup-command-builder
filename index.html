    Max heapFixed heapShrinking heapRegion sizeGood for
    
    
    Shrinking heap starts at a quarter of the maximum and gives memory back when idle. Fixed heap claims everything at startup, which is steadier on a dedicated PC. Above 12 GB, Aikar's larger-heap values apply (40/50% new size, 16M regions, 15% reserve, 20% occupancy). The 4M region size at 4 GB and below is an adjustment, not Aikar's own figure.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    More is not faster. A bigger heap just makes each full cleanup longer. Pick the smallest -Xmx your peak load fits in.
    
    
    Measure, do not guess. Run spark healthreport with your biggest group online. If the heap sits under about 70% of the max, you have enough.
    
    
    Leave room outside the heap. Java uses another 1 to 2 GB beyond -Xmx on a heavily modded server, and your OS needs RAM too. On a 24 GB PC, 8 GB is a good ceiling.
    
    
    Test it. The builder gives you flags, not a guarantee. Watch the server log for warnings the first time you start.
